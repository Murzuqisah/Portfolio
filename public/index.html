About Me

Joel is a full-stack software developer who's spent the last two years building modern, human-centered web applications. Coming from a strong foundation in IT hardware, software development, and systems security, he approaches problems with the mindset of someone who understands tech from the metal all the way to the user interface.

He's worked on everything from AI-powered marketplaces and financial dashboards to education and accessibility platforms — often taking on both frontend and backend responsibilities. His go-to stack includes Go, JavaScript, PHP (Laravel), Inertia.js, TypeScript, and TailwindCSS, which he uses to craft reliable, scalable, and clean digital experiences.

Joel currently builds as a freelancer, creating secure, real-world solutions while sharpening his expertise in cybersecurity and blockchain. He's especially invested in technologies that boost transparency, trust, and data protection — the stuff that keeps systems honest and users safe.

At the end of the day, whether he's shipping clean code, tightening security, or picking up a new skill to level up, Joel is driven by one clear goal: build tech that actually matters.
